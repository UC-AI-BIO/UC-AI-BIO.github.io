Joshua Metcalf is a Ph.D. student in Aerospace Engineering at the University of Cincinnati and a graduate researcher in the AI Bio Lab. He earned his B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, with a minor in Aerospace Engineering, from Trine University and is concurrently completing his M.S. at UC. His research focuses on interpretable and explainable artificial intelligence for healthcare, with particular emphasis on fuzzy inference systems, evolutionary optimization, and clinical classification. His current work applies Pittsburgh Genetic Algorithm-evolved Takagi-Sugeno-Kang fuzzy systems to breast cancer Fine Needle Aspiration data, with the goal of developing accurate classification systems that remain interpretable for clinical use. Alongside his research, Joshua serves as a computer science faculty member at Mount St. Joseph University, where he teaches Python programming and introductory artificial intelligence. His background also includes engineering industry experience in fire protection system design.

Lynn Pickering successfully defended her Ph.D. in June 2025. Her thesis, *The lenses of interpretability: a framework for investigating the interpretability of learned models*, was jointly advised with Prof. Bernard De Baets of Ghent University and received the Best PhD Thesis Award at NAFIPS 2025.
